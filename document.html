The comparison canonicalizes raw signed-16-bit XYZ triangle positions and preserves multiplicity. It ignores winding, triangle order, normals, UVs, weights, and materials. Matching geometry is evidence of shared positions, not identical files, textures, rigs, or native actor behavior. The model search covers 365 unique GOG EVX members, 3,922 KMY members for source digests, and bounded stored Trial catalogs. None of the eight new source-member hashes matches those GOG or Trial inventories.

The four compared Trial occurrences of Snake's 55aaa5 resource share 2,995 of 3,002 triangles with Document, approximately 99.77%. Their position multisets differ. In the GOG name catalog, 55aaa5 and 09274e each resolve to one observed basename candidate; these are bounded name leads, not recovered Document filenames.
