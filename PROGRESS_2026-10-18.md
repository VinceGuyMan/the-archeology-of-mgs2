## GOG: from a pose to reusable specimens

The newer body-cycle specimen covers all 1,932 triangles across 22 parts and two 141-key actions, with Blender import, save and fresh-process reopen checks. A separate arms study covers three actions and 321 source samples with qualified base texture/UV export. These are specific, independently checked offline specimens, not a complete game exporter.

The first extra-material attempts remain recorded with their failures: standard Blender normal storage missed the fixed precision criterion, and an early full-float qualification changed existing base normals. A later source-corrected version passed the full 321-sample Blender import/reopen gate; all 246 bound input/output hashes were verified. Its immutable bundle retains the earlier failed route, frozen fixtures, contracts, math/GPU receipts, and output hashes. The standard glTF import limitation remains explicit, and native appearance, timing, and GPU parity are not claimed.

The optional extra-material mode was added to the arms inspector while keeping neutral startup and the base preview intact. The qualification report records 12 UI checks at desktop and 390 px widths, covering three clips, play/seek, camera orbit/reset, shader controls, material restoration, and failure fallbacks.

The GOG rigid cohort is now 100% qualified for both geometry and material/texture mapping across all 45 models (7,136 vertices, 5,201 triangles), verified against `weapon_nm.xti`, `transresident.xti`, and `title.qar`. All 12 alternate layouts in `resident.dar` have been classified under a fail-closed gate: one proven skinned candidate (`sna_def.kmy`, 22 parts, dual-action 141-key motion export verified in Blender with fresh-process reopen), three glass overlays (stride 20, flags 18433, material IDs 0x48/0x268 blocked on dynamic alpha-blending and environment reflection buffers), five cardboard box models (stride 28, packed vertex attributes and procedural damage states), two stencil shadow projection volumes, and one dummy skeleton kinematic carrier (55 parts). Codec authoring now supports editable dialogue, imported PCM audio, save/reopen, preview seeking and checked video export. Desktop and narrow-screen checks and two independently checked movies establish a useful standalone production foundation. Microphone recording, voice acting and original-game Codec behavior remain unproven.

## Skateboarding: a separate excavation

The skate mode has its own PS2 executable, `SKATE2.ELF`, and a `BNDR` archive with 115 named records. Directory offsets and sizes permit deterministic member extraction. This establishes an asset/code research route, not a faithful browser runtime. The public catalog includes an original procedural board proxy for interaction; it is not a recovered game model. No skate model geometry is bundled.

Stage/collision semantics, movement arithmetic, input behavior and original-game fidelity require their own evidence. A playable original-inspired prototype, a static capture and a bounded instruction replay are different outcomes. We do not label any of them a complete recreation. The preserved skate project remains paused.
