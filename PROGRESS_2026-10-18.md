# Field notes — October 18, 2026

## GOG: from a pose to reusable specimens

The newer body-cycle specimen covers all 1,932 triangles across 22 parts and two 141-key actions, with Blender import, save and fresh-process reopen checks. A separate arms study covers three actions and 321 source samples with qualified base texture/UV export. These are specific, independently checked offline specimens, not a complete game exporter.

Extra material stages are a separate work in progress: source calculations for 204 triangles and synthetic WebGL readback tests passed. Standard Blender normal storage failed the fixed precision criterion. A full-float approach passed a rest/save/reopen probe and one posed test, but its first complete qualification failed because it changed existing base normals. A later diagnostic isolated the normal-read policy responsible. The correction and full 321-sample/reopen qualification remain pending. We preserve failures instead of loosening the criteria.

Codec authoring now supports editable dialogue, imported PCM audio, save/reopen, preview seeking and checked video export. Desktop and narrow-screen checks and two independently checked movies establish a useful standalone production foundation. Microphone recording, voice acting and original-game Codec behavior remain unproven.

## PS2 and trial: preserved discoveries

The trial project has source inventories, actor/model/texture research, native traces and separately labeled modification candidates. Its retained checkpoint reports 464 repository tests passing. Those offline tests do not validate newly built character-picker, Craig or cumulative cinematic candidates inside the game. Working historical builds and newer untested candidates remain distinct.

Disassembly and pseudocode are analysis products, not recovered original source or proof of complete engine understanding. Edition identity must follow executable/disc identifiers and hashes, not directory labels. PS2 and trial experiments remain paused; these notes do not restart them or imply original-GOG compatibility.

## Skateboarding: a separate excavation

The skate mode has its own PS2 executable, `SKATE2.ELF`, and a `BNDR` archive with 115 named records. Directory offsets and sizes permit deterministic member extraction. This establishes an asset/code research route, not a faithful browser runtime.

Stage/collision semantics, movement arithmetic, input behavior and original-game fidelity require their own evidence. A playable original-inspired prototype, a static capture and a bounded instruction replay are different outcomes. We do not label any of them a complete recreation. The preserved skate project remains paused; dedicated interactive museum rooms are still planned.
